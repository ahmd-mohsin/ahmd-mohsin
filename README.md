## About

PhD student in Electrical Engineering at **Stanford**, co-advised by **Dr. Emily Fox** and **Dr. John M. Cioffi**. I work on reinforcement learning for verifiable rewards, preference optimization, test-time scaling, and agentic systems for reliable reasoning models. ## Research

### Preference Optimization & Test-Time Scaling
Designing sample-efficient preference-learning and reinforcement-learning methods for aligning language-model reasoning, and adaptive test-time compute methods that dynamically control inference depth, tool invocation, and verification under strict budgets. I study how post-training and inference-time search jointly shape reasoning, and the principled trade-offs among accuracy, latency, and reliability.
> `General Preference RL` · `Continuous-Utility DPO` · `Active Bayesian Preference Models` · `Adaptive test-time compute` · `Coverage-preserving RL`

### Agentic System Architectures & Coding Agents
Building agentic systems that plan, act, and self-verify over long horizons, spanning multi-agent coordination and the software agents I develop at alexein.ai. Through my work at **Amazon AGI**, I study how coding agents convert test-time compute into solved tasks by exploring diverse solutions and writing trustworthy verifiers, and how these behaviors are internalized through supervised and reinforcement post-training.
> `Teaching Agents to Code Reliably` · `SWE-bench Verified` · `Self-verifiers via GRPO` · `Multi-agent coordination`

### AI Alignment & Benchmarking
Developing persona-based benchmarks and user-simulation frameworks that evaluate how models behave across diverse populations and interactive settings. I am broadly interested in alignment, including calibrated uncertainty and reducing sycophancy, and in measurement methodology that makes alignment progress observable and reproducible.
> `PersonaEval` · `MatrAIx` · `Sycophancy disentanglement` · `Reward decomposition`
