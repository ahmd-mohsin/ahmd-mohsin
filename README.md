## Research

<table>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

**Preference Optimization & Test-Time Scaling**

Sample-efficient preference-learning and RL methods for aligning
language-model reasoning, plus adaptive test-time compute that controls
inference depth, tool invocation, and verification under strict budgets.
I study how post-training and inference-time search jointly shape
reasoning, and the trade-offs among accuracy, latency, and reliability.

`General Preference RL` `Continuous-Utility DPO` `Adaptive compute` `Coverage-preserving RL`

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

**Agentic System Architectures & Coding Agents**

Agentic systems that plan, act, and self-verify over long horizons,
spanning multi-agent coordination and the software agents I develop at
alexein.ai. At **Amazon AGI** I study how coding agents convert test-time
compute into solved tasks by exploring diverse solutions and writing
trustworthy verifiers, and how those behaviors are internalized through
supervised and reinforcement post-training.

`Teaching Agents to Code Reliably` `SWE-bench Verified` `GRPO self-verifiers`

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

**AI Alignment & Benchmarking**

Persona-based benchmarks and user-simulation frameworks that evaluate how
models behave across diverse populations and interactive settings. I care
about calibrated uncertainty, reducing sycophancy, and measurement
methodology that makes alignment progress observable and reproducible.

`PersonaEval` `MatrAIx` `Sycophancy disentanglement` `Reward decomposition`

</td>
</tr>
</table>
